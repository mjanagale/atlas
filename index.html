About ATLAS

Global trade has many moving parts. We bring them together.

ATLAS Global Import and Export is an international trade, sourcing and logistics company based in Yiwu, China. We connect businesses and customers with manufacturers, suppliers and commercial opportunities across major Asian markets, with a practical focus on China–Africa trade.

Our work combines supplier sourcing, purchasing coordination, cargo consolidation and international shipping support, so cross-border trade is simpler from supplier to destination.

Our vision

To build a trusted international trade and logistics platform that connects African businesses and customers with reliable global supply markets.

Our mission

To provide practical, transparent and cost-conscious import, export, sourcing and cargo solutions, while building long-term relationships with customers, suppliers and logistics partners.

What we do

Practical support at every step.

Whether it's a first order or regular commercial cargo, we connect the details from supplier to shipment.

International cargo & shipping

We coordinate cargo movement from supplier locations in China and other sourcing markets to destination markets.

LCL / groupage cargo

Smaller shipments from different suppliers are consolidated into shared cargo, so you can ship without a full container.

FCL / container shipping

For larger commercial shipments, we coordinate full-container cargo preparation and shipping arrangements.

Sourcing & procurement

We help you identify suppliers, compare products and coordinate purchasing from Chinese markets.

Supplier & order coordination

Supplier communication, collection, consolidation and shipment preparation, managed across multiple vendors.

Import & export support

Commercial and logistics coordination to help move your products across borders with fewer surprises.

How we work

Clear steps. Coordinated details.

Every request is different. We learn the details and coordinate the steps that fit your order.

  1. 01

    Share your request

    Send product details, quantities, specifications or supplier information.

  2. 02

    Source & coordinate

    We identify suppliers or coordinate with the suppliers you have selected.

  3. 03

    Purchase & collect

    Orders are coordinated and goods are arranged for collection or warehouse delivery.

  4. 04

    Consolidate & prepare

    Where applicable, goods from multiple suppliers are consolidated and prepared for shipment.

  5. 05

    Ship internationally

    Cargo is arranged for the agreed shipping method and destination.

  6. 06

    Stay informed

    We keep you updated on cargo status. You can also track it online with your reference.
